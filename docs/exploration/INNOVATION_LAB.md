# Innovation lab — non-binding
Business DNA: structured profile tentang capability, offering, goals, constraints dan history; risiko profiling berlebihan, validasi melalui onboarding sederhana. Adaptive workspace: conditional UI berdasarkan capability, bukan autonomous schema. Health score: rule-based transparent, jangan menyembunyikan weighting atau mengklaim diagnosis. Growth planner: opsi dengan asumsi, bukan janji laba. Opportunity discovery: evidence dan source date. Daily briefing: metric snapshot dan actionable next steps. Campaign orchestration: draft, approval, per-channel status. Digital twin: scenario model, bukan representasi pasti usaha. AI Business Partner: satu identity lintas studio, assistant/copilot dahulu; agent actions terbatas approval. Semua PROPOSED/EXPLORATORY, bukan backlog wajib. Setiap eksperimen punya hypothesis, success criterion, cost ceiling, privacy review dan stop condition sebelum dimulai.
